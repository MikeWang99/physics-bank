# Physics Bank v2.1.0

- Adds canonical per-question `year` metadata.
- Adds matching `year:YYYY` tags for filtering.
- Adds `source.year` and `year_evidence` provenance.
- Adds deterministic year stamping from explicit metadata or source filenames.
- Treats missing or ambiguous years as manual-review items rather than guessing.
- Adds a dedicated year metadata validator and regression tests.
